About

Veteran-owned procurement built for federal customers.

Cascade Contracting Group is the operating brand of Cascade Procurement Group LLC, a Washington-based procurement and supply company focused on delivering commercial products and sourcing solutions to government and commercial customers. The company is 100% owned and operated by a service-disabled U.S. military veteran and is pursuing SBA Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) certification.

Company leadership brings prior experience in molecular cytology field engineering and the VA healthcare environment, supporting a focused approach to medical, laboratory, diagnostic, technical, and mission-critical supply opportunities.
